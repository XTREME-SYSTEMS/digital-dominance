# Eden Skye | Universal AI Vision-to-Delivery Contract

**Status: draft prototype, isolated from public Website Launch Engine and existing production.** The browser demo is an *offline local prototype*. It does not call models, store leads, send messages, connect voice, book meetings, charge cards or provision software.

## Product principle
Welcome the person's idea without assuming the full implementation is feasible, safe or affordable. Eden suggests one or more service families and shapes an attainable first phase. A budget-limited founder can order a discovery blueprint without the misleading promise of a complete app at discovery-sprint pricing. No unsourced competitor price comparisons.

## Company service catalog
The catalog is deliberately broad and open to new, versioned offerings. Families: Software; AI Agents; Automation; Intelligence; Consulting; Creative; Growth; Custom. Every new category requires supported capabilities, licensed inputs, real delivery time, safe compliance boundaries and maintainable support commitments. Accept new ideas for *evaluation*; do not blindly auto-promise any service.

## Intake and privacy
Eden prompts: vision in the client's own words, proposed service, desired result, essential modules, outside apps, user population, sensitive/regulated data, budget range and timeline. Only ask identity/contact after consent for follow-up. Server verifies identity, consent, rate limits, anti-abuse checks and tenant scope before durable saves. No uploaded customer data is placed inside shared sandbox jobs. Voice requires approved voice and microphone permissions, privacy notice and call-consent handling.

## Pricing policy
Pricing is deterministic and currently uses **unvalidated internal assumptions** ($45/hour assumed delivery cost, 1.2x overhead and minimum modeled gross margin 30%). These are NOT actual observed costs, competitor quotes, public prices or approved client tariffs. Outputs are indicative, not legally binding. Quote fingerprints in browser are deterministic preview labels, not signed receipts.

Price formula: estimated scoped hours x assumed loaded cost per hour x overhead / (1 - minimum margin), rounded up, with a 30% upper variance band. Real quotes require feasibility review, vendor spend calculation, client acceptance, delivery scope, payment terms, tax and refund policy, supported timeline and a cryptographically signed immutable quote from the backend.

Escalate to discovery for compliance-sensitive, novel custom, enterprise or integration-heavy requests. No automatic commitments for medical/legal/financial regulated implementations, unsafe data extraction, prohibited actions, production credentials or unauthorized third-party systems.

## Deployment integration path
1. **READ**: browser intake and suggestions; synthetic quote only, no contact data collection.
2. **DRAFT**: authenticated server stores consented intake in existing X1 Staging `dominance_onboarding_sessions` for synthetic integration testing; map to Strategic Intelligence using a reviewed tenant-scoped adapter only after RLS issues are fixed. Do not mirror full personal data to four Supabase projects.
3. **QUOTE**: back-office scope and effort engine, cost table version, validated delivery adapters; state `INDICATIVE -> REVIEWED -> OFFERED -> ACCEPTED -> FUNDED` with immutable receipts.
4. **BUILD**: only accepted and funded work routes into existing authoritative X1 queue, sandbox fleet, approved GitHub worktree, Vercel preview, Railway worker and independent QA.
5. **DELIVER**: approved production deployment, client portal, support SLO and verified artifacts in approved Google Drive. Each client has own tenant, data segregation, offboarding and billing contract.

Standing sandbox envelopes may automate *scoped* reversible code operations, but do not implicitly authorize DNS, production releases, client communication, secrets, spend, real payment or destructive operations outside sandbox ownership. Reuse one X1 supervisor and existing queues; do not create new production queues, crons or platform control planes.

## Service delivery models
- Idea-to-Blueprint Sprint: clarify outcome, feasibility, scope, prototype concept, phased proposal, not a full custom app.
- Productized MVP: reuse premium secure templates and connectors, deliver minimum actual customer value at transparent bounded scope.
- Managed Growth/Business OS: ongoing support, automation, analytics, compliance monitoring and measurable recurring value.
- Custom/Enterprise: scoped architect review, data/governance review, managed execution and change controls.
