## Product behavior

1. Show all 10 designs as **actual approved PNGs** in an XPS gallery rather than stock-template substitutions.
2. Clicking a design sets the active template ID. Provide desktop/mobile full-page reference and an editable implementation preview, clearly distinguished.
3. Preserve the approved layout, photography positions, and visual design. Apply the approved National Epoxy Pros gold USA-network logo.
4. Editable business content: company name, headline, description, approved logo, verified phone/email, service areas, contact CTA, service details, and original first-party media.
5. Changes affect the implementation preview, never mutate the original approved reference. Store per-template edits as draft configuration.
6. Download HTML and a 1/10/100 preview batch manifest. Batch generation must stay noindex and require explicit launch approval.
7. First functional implementation target: design 01, with design 06 providing the Digital Bid flow. Bind BidFast only after authenticated staging integration and independent tests.
8. Reuse the existing Digital Dominance registry, preview and validation pipeline, including its single authorized X1 queue/supervisor. Do not create a competing control plane.
