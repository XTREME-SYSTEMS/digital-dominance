## Visual and operational quality contract
- Do not promote a concept from review into the approved template library without visual operator selection and independent validation. No mass 200/200 run before a real premium master passes.
- Assets: purpose-specific photography; capture source pages, image SHA-256 and use rights. Users must be told where imagery is illustrative. No unrelated category substitutions.
- Different IA for each industry: not one skeleton with palette changes. Site screenshots must show distinct hero/composition, section families, CTAs, and interaction contracts.
- At 1440 desktop, 768 tablet, 390 mobile: image presence, zero overflow, working links and controls, accessible keyboard focus. Full-page screenshots plus independent visual review mandatory.
- >=99% reference parity is only meaningful after a specific approved design reference and measurement method are locked. At concept stage, aesthetic parity is NOT MEASURED.
- Declared operational tests require 100% PASS; actual lead delivery, CRM, auth, tenant security, RLS, production deployment, Google indexing and revenue remain UNKNOWN.
- The preview demo must not solicit personal details, send leads, represent a provider or publish a site.
- Search quality: avoid scaled thin content, doorway city pages, fake reviews, fabricated credentials, unsupported claims, fake location presence.
- A11y review pending: contrast and focus checks, semantics, motion, text scaling and screen reader tests. No WCAG certification claimed.
