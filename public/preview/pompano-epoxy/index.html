COMMON QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED.

Is the estimate a final price?

No. This preview shows a demonstration range based on internal test assumptions. A real quote depends on measured area, repairs, coating specifications and site inspection.

Do photos get sent anywhere?

Not in this development preview. The browser checks selection count and file size, but does not upload or transmit the files.

Why does moisture testing matter?

Concrete moisture and existing contamination can affect coating performance. Ask your installer about appropriate testing and preparation.

Can I request a real installation appointment?

Live booking and CRM submission are intentionally disabled here pending secure integration testing and approval.
