Before you begin

Your questions, answered.

What matters when choosing a floor coating?

Concrete condition, moisture, preparation, intended use and curing requirements all matter. Ask how the surface will be assessed and prepared before selecting an epoxy or polyaspartic system.

How do I choose the right service?

Start with your priorities and review the epoxy flooring service options. A consultation can clarify scope, suitability and the next step.

What affects the cost?

Scope, materials, timing, complexity and individual requirements can affect pricing. Request a written proposal rather than relying on a general estimate.

What should I prepare before a consultation?

Bring a description of your goals, relevant details and questions. Avoid sending sensitive information through an unverified channel.

How can I confirm availability?

Contact the business directly to confirm its current coverage, schedule, qualifications and service terms.
